2,295 messaging conversations through Meta Ads

In the period 24 October–22 November 2026, three messaging campaigns generated 2,295 conversations for a luxury curtain showroom in Bangladesh.

CAMPAIGN PERFORMANCEReal campaign screenshotOriginal image to be added before publication
THE STRATEGY

Turning interest into action.

Over approximately four months, we combined broad targeting and retargeting. We began by introducing the showroom to a broad audience, then built custom audiences from people who engaged with the ads. Meta Pixel tracking helped us build a website visitor audience. Retargeting ad sets gave the showroom another opportunity to reconnect with interested people and encourage inquiries.
