THE STRATEGY

Turning interest into action.

The client wanted more people to discover his content, engage with videos, and visit his profile. Over approximately three months, we combined reach, content interaction, profile visit and follower campaigns. We promoted video, personal and UGC-style content to introduce the professional to new audiences and encourage viewers to engage and explore his profile.
